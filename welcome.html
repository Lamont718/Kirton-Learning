The first five business days

Nothing is needed from you except the IEP, and I'll send you a private way to send it.

  1. A private upload link, by email

    It arrives from me after checkout. The IEP goes straight into an encrypted folder only I can open. Never send it over regular email, and never to this website — if anything asks you to, it isn't me.

  2. I read the whole document

    Every academic goal, the present levels, and the accommodations on page eight. Five business days.

  3. The Blueprint, then a call

    Thirty minutes, on the phone, where I walk you through what I found and what the year looks like. Bring questions. Bring the parts of the IEP nobody has ever explained.

If you change your mind, tell me within fourteen days of getting your Blueprint and I refund it in full. You keep the Blueprint either way.

Where the work actually happens

Your child's work runs in a separate app. It is built around the goals in her IEP, at the level the goal is written at, with the accommodations the document already calls for.

The work

Open the app

Put her goals in word for word — or open the IEP and let it read them out for you — pick which engine works each one, and she can start. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account.

kirton-learn.vercel.app

Two things worth knowing on day one

  • Use one device. Everything lives in the browser you use, so work done on a phone is not on the tablet. If you have already used two, save a copy from each and use Restore — it offers to add them together rather than replace.
  • Save a copy now and then. The record is on your device and nowhere else, which is the point — and it means a cleared browser takes it with it. The record page has a button for it.

If something looks wrong

  • "Nothing is being saved on this device." Your browser is refusing to store anything — usually a private window, or site data blocked for that address. Save a copy to a file before you close the tab, then open the app in a normal window.
  • "Something is wrong with the saved record." Nothing has been deleted. Save the damaged file first, then restore from a copy you kept. Send me both and I'll look.

Sending me the record

The record never leaves your device on its own. When I need to see how the year is going, I ask for it — and you decide whether to send it.

When

Before each of our calls, and before an IEP meeting if you'd like me to read it first.

What to send

Open The record in the app, set the date range to the period we're talking about, and use Print — then save it as a PDF instead of printing it.

How

Through a private link I send you, the same kind you used for the IEP. Never over regular email. It has her name and her goals in it, word for word, and it deserves the same care the IEP gets.

What I don't do

I work the academic goals — reading, writing and math. Speech, occupational therapy, physical therapy, counseling and behavior support are licensed professions and I am not licensed in any of them, so I cannot work those goals at any price.

Most IEPs hold both kinds. That does not make your child a bad fit — it means she has more than one person on her team. When you open her IEP in the app, it will tell you which goals are which.

Anything at all

If you are stuck, if something in the app is confusing, if the work is too hard or too easy, or if a week went badly — tell me. Especially if a week went badly. That is the part I need to hear about, and it is the part people go quiet about.
