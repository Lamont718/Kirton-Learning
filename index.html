How you'll know

You shouldn't have to take my word for whether it's working.

Before I teach anything, I measure where your child is right now — using the same measure the goal itself is written in. Everything after that gets compared to that starting point.

01 — The baseline

Week 0, before any teaching

What your child can do today, written down and dated. Without it, every number later is just a number.

02 — The checks

Cold, and on a fixed schedule

Decoding every week; comprehension and math on even weeks; writing on odd. Always using work they have never seen — never the material they practiced that week.

03 — The rule

Four flat checks and the teaching changes

Not the goal. The criterion in the IEP doesn't soften because the work got hard — and you hear it from me on the monthly call before you read it in the Log.

See the whole measurement design

The practice your child does during the week is coached, so it never counts as a score — only the cold checks do. This is collected at your kitchen table by you and me, not by an evaluator, and it does not replace the progress reporting your school does.

Straight answers

The questions parents actually ask me.

Who sees my child's IEP?

Only me. It goes into an encrypted folder, it is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with your school or anyone else. Ask me to delete it and I delete it and confirm in writing. I'll never ask you to send it over regular email or upload it to this page.

Is this special education advocacy or legal help?

No. I'm not an attorney or a certified advocate, I don't represent you at IEP meetings, and nothing I write is legal advice. What I give you is a clear read of the goals and a written record of your child's work — which tends to make you a much better advocate for yourself.

Does this replace what the school has to provide?

No, and don't let anyone tell you it does. Your child's school is legally required to deliver the services on that IEP. Kirton Learning is supplemental — it's what happens at your kitchen table, not instead of what happens at school.

Do you diagnose anything, or decide what services my child qualifies for?

Never. Diagnosis and eligibility are decisions for licensed professionals and your child's IEP team. I work from the goals that are already written.

My child isn't autistic — does this still work?

Yes. I build from whatever the IEP says, whether that's ADHD, dyslexia, a speech or language impairment, or anything else. The method doesn't care about the label; it cares about the goals and what your child is into.

What ages?

K–12. It runs strongest with 2nd through 8th grade, where a kid is old enough to run a build and young enough that the habit sticks.

How much time does my child need to put in?

Three to four sessions a week, 20 to 40 minutes each, depending on age. It's designed around the reality that you're tired and the evening is short.

How will I know if it's actually working?

Before I teach anything I measure where your child is, written the same way the goal is written, and everything after that gets compared to that starting point. The checks run on a fixed schedule using material your child has never seen — the coached practice they do during the week never counts as a score. If four checks in a row come back flat, I change how I'm teaching it, not the goal, and you hear that from me on the monthly call before you read it in the Log. You can see the entire design in the worked example. What I won't do is promise you a result.

What if it isn't a fit?

Tell me within 14 days of getting your Blueprint and I refund it in full. Keep the Blueprint either way.
