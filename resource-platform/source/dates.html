What this tool does and doesn't do

It does date arithmetic against published rules, and tells you which committee handles a child of that age. That's all it is — a calendar, not an assessment.

It doesn't know anything about your child, doesn't tell you what services to seek, and can't tell you whether a delay in your case is a problem. For that, call the offices above.
