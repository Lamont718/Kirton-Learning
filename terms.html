What Kirton Learning is

Kirton Learning is supplemental educational coaching. I read the goals already written in your child's IEP, translate them into plain English, and design project-based work — expeditions, weekly lessons, and a written log — that is aligned to those goals.

What Kirton Learning is not. This is the part I want you to read twice, because some services in this space are vague about it and you deserve better than vague.

  • Not special education advocacy. I am not a certified special education advocate. I don't represent you at IEP meetings, CSE meetings, mediations, or hearings.
  • Not legal advice. I am not an attorney. Nothing I write or say is legal advice, and reading it doesn't create an attorney-client relationship.
  • Not diagnosis or evaluation. I don't diagnose anything, evaluate your child, or determine what services or classifications your child qualifies for. Those are decisions for licensed professionals and your child's IEP team.
  • Not medical advice or therapy. Kirton Learning is not special education instruction, related services, speech therapy, occupational therapy, counseling, or any clinical service.
  • Not a replacement for your school. Your school district is legally required to deliver the services on your child's IEP. Kirton Learning does not deliver, replace, satisfy, or discharge any part of that obligation. If your district isn't delivering, Kirton Learning is not the remedy — a lawyer or a certified advocate is.

Kirton Learning is what happens at your kitchen table. It is in addition to school, never instead of it.

What I deliver

The Blueprint — $349, one time

  • Every goal in your child's IEP translated into plain English.
  • A 36-week skill map across reading, writing, and math, aligned to those goals.
  • The first build expedition, designed around something your child cares about.
  • A 30-minute walkthrough call with me.
  • Delivered within 5 business days of receiving both your IEP upload and your completed intake form. If I'm going to miss that, I'll tell you before the deadline, not after.

Kirton Learning Family — $149 per month

  • Everything in the Blueprint.
  • Weekly lessons built on the current expedition, emailed to you.
  • The Log — a dated record of completed work.
  • A monthly 30-minute call to adjust the plan.
  • A new expedition each term.
