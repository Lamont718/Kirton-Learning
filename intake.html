Six questions

Tell me about your kid.

The IEP tells me what the district wrote down. It does not tell me who they are. Six questions, about five minutes, and the answers shape the first month of work more than the document does.

This page needs the private link I emailed you after checkout. Open it from that email, or email me and I’ll send a fresh one.

Reply to the email I sent you, or write to lamont@kirtonlearning.com, and I’ll sort it out. It costs you nothing and it is normal.

Opening your form…

First name only. I don’t need a surname and I’d rather not hold one.

Write it however the school writes it — 3rd, K, 12:1:1, ungraded. All of those are fine.

The next two are about reading, writing, and math — that is the whole of what I work on. For speech, OT, counseling, or behavior, I’m not the person, and I’ll tell you who to call. Say whatever you want to say here; I just don’t want you spending your five minutes on the parts I can’t help with.

In reading, writing, or math. Anything they can do now that they couldn’t a year ago counts, however small it sounds written down.

Same three. The specific thing, if you have it — “reads it fine and can’t tell me what it said” tells me more than “struggles with reading.”

This is the one I build from. Trains, a game, a show they quote at you, a dog, a person. If they’ve talked about the same thing for two years, that is the answer — put it here.

Phone, email, text — and the hours that actually work. I’d rather call at a good time than three times at a bad one.

 

This goes into the same encrypted place as the IEP, it is used only to build your child’s plan, and it is deleted with everything else whenever you ask. Nothing you type here is sent to an analytics service, an ad network, or anyone else.

Got it. Your answers are in.

What I do with this

  • I read it next to the IEP. Where the document and the answers disagree, that gap is usually the useful part.
  • The first build gets designed around what you put in the third box, not around the diagnosis.
  • If something you wrote needs a conversation rather than a plan, I’ll raise it on our call.

Nothing else is needed from you right now. You can close this page. If you left something out or want to change an answer, just reply to my email — that reaches me directly.
